## 🎯 OS 3 PROJETOS EDUCACIONAIS

### **PROJETO #1: Churn Prediction (Retenção)**
**Domínio:** Qualquer empresa (SaaS, Streaming, Telecom)  
**Dados:** 10k clientes fictícios (realistas)  
**Problema:** Qual cliente vai sair? Por quê? Como reter?

**Conceitos cobertos:**
- ✅ Week 2: Structured Outputs (output schema)
- ✅ Week 3: RAG Traditional (retrieves similar churned customers)
- ✅ Week 4: Adaptive RAG (decides if needs retrieval)
- ✅ Week 5: Tool Use (query database)
- ✅ Week 6: Agentic Loops (try 3 strategies, stop at 0.85 confidence)
- ✅ Week 7: LangGraph (linear pipeline)
- ✅ Week 7: Confidence Scoring (escalate if < 0.60)
- ✅ Week 8: Observability (track prediction accuracy)
- ✅ Week 9: Evaluation (test vs actual churn after 30d)
- ✅ Week 10: Deployment (Docker + GCP Cloud Run)
- ✅ Week 11: Cost tracking (per prediction)

**Stack:** Python + LangGraph + Pydantic + Firestore + Claude
**Status:** Production-ready
